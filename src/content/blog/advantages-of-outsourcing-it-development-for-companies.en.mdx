While this scenario often represents the ideal for a company's **digital development**, it can be costly to have a team of developers dedicated to maintaining and developing IT systems. When discussing [outsourcing IT development or facilities management](/en-ca/services/devops/outsourcing-application-development-and-maintenance), we think a company or organization can transfer this workload to an external partner. Cost reduction, specialized expertise, resource flexibility: this practice is winning over more and more companies.

## Outsourcing to cut costs

Of course, the first benefit that comes to mind when talking about **outsourcing or facilities management** is cost reduction. Indeed, while this workload transfer represents an investment, it is also a fast and effective way of reducing a company's operating costs.

By outsourcing IT development, a company can significantly reduce its investment in IT infrastructure. For example, the purchase of hardware, software or licenses, which can sometimes prove costly, can be reduced, as can the associated payroll costs (salaries, training, benefits, etc.). However, the external partner's employees will manage IT development and maintenance excellently.

Rest assured, if the in-house team does not handle IT development and maintenance, this part remains in good hands since outsourcing provides access to qualified expertise.

By entrusting development to a facilities management company or a web agency like Nmédia, a company can be sure of having direct access to a talented team that will ensure continuity and security of services quickly.

## Outsourcing for greater flexibility

Another significant advantage of outsourcing is its flexibility. **By entrusting outsourcing to an external company**, you can quickly and easily adjust the size of your payroll resources according to your company's needs, whether the workload is increasing or decreasing. Thanks to the expertise of external teams, it also saves considerable time when completing projects to tight deadlines.
